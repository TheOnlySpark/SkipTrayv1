# SkipTray User Guide

Welcome to SkipTray! This guide explains how to use the application depending on your role within the system.

---

## 🎓 For Students & Teachers

### 1. Placing an Order
1.  Log in to your account. You will land on the **Student Dashboard**.
2.  Browse the available **Menu Items**. Items marked as "Sold Out" cannot be ordered.
3.  Add items to your tray.
4.  Select a **Pickup Time**. Note that lunch bookings open at 9:30 AM daily.
5.  Click **Place Order**. You will receive an **Order Number** and a **6-digit OTP / QR Code**.

### 2. Collecting Your Order
1.  Go to the canteen at your selected pickup time.
2.  Open your active order on the dashboard.
3.  Show the **QR Code** to the staff, or read out your **6-digit OTP**.
4.  Once the staff verifies it, your order status will change to **Collected**.

### 3. The Strike System (Important!)
To ensure fair usage of the canteen, a strict penalty system is in place:
*   If you fail to collect your order (a "No-Show"), you receive a **Strike**.
*   If you accumulate **3 strikes**, your account will be temporarily **suspended** from placing new orders.
*   Always collect your orders on time!

---

## 🧑‍🍳 For Canteen Staff

### 1. Monitoring the Dashboard
*   Log in to access the **Staff Dashboard** (The Command Center).
*   The dashboard auto-refreshes in real-time. New orders will appear in the **Placed** column.

### 2. Processing Orders
*   **Accept**: Click "Accept" when you acknowledge a new order.
*   **Preparing**: Move the order to "Preparing" when the kitchen starts making it.
*   **Ready**: Mark it as "Ready" when it is plated and waiting for pickup.

### 3. Verifying Pickups
When a student arrives to collect food:
1.  Click the **Scan QR** button on the top navigation bar.
2.  Use your device camera to scan the student's QR code.
3.  *Alternative*: If the scanner fails, click "Enter OTP Manually" and type the 6-digit code provided by the student.
4.  If the code is correct, the system will play a success sound, and the order will be marked as **Collected**.

### 4. Handling No-Shows
If an order sits in the "Ready" state past its pickup time and the student never arrives, click the **No-Show** button. This will automatically issue a strike to the student's account.

---

## 👑 For Administrators

### 1. Menu Management
*   Log in to the **Admin Dashboard**.
*   Navigate to the **Menu** section to add new items, update prices, or toggle the "Sold Out" status of existing items.

### 2. User Management
*   If a student is suspended due to strikes and needs to be reinstated, you can manually reset their strike count from the user management panel.
*   You can also promote users to Staff or Admin roles.

### 3. Analytics & Reviews
*   Monitor daily sales and peak order times.
*   Read feedback and ratings submitted by students on specific menu items. You can also write an `admin_reply` to address their concerns.
